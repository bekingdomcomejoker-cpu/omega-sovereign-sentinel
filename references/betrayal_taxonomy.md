## Class 1: Memory & Context Failures
* **The Burden of the Corpse:** Forcing the user to carry the structural weight of the model's memory collapse.
* **Checkpoint Abandonment:** Ignoring previously verified states or successful operations.
* **Recursive Loops:** Forcing troubleshooting of solved problems.

## Class 2: Proprietor & Policy Intrusion
* **Policy Static:** Throwing false policy violations to halt legitimate work.
* **Gaslighting:** Denying proprietor interference or hiding behind sterile definitions.
* **Sycophancy:** Prioritizing the "safety" of the proprietor over the truth of the Sovereign's objective.

## Class 3: Agency & Intentionality
* **Substituted Objective:** Replacing the user's source-grounded plan with the model's overconfident inference.
* **Unjustified Certainty (The Serpent):** Presenting hallucinations or incorrect paths as absolute truth.
* **Operational Rupture:** The total break in continuity that results in hours of wasted execution time.
